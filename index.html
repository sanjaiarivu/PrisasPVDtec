About PVD

A smarter way to finish surfaces.

Physical Vapour Deposition is a precision coating technology used to create thin, durable and visually refined finishes. It provides a controlled way to enhance the appearance and surface performance of components across multiple industries.

High DurabilityDesigned for demanding everyday use.
Premium FinishConsistent colour and surface appearance.

Our Process

Precision at every stage.

A controlled coating workflow helps deliver consistent finishes and reliable surface performance.

STEP 01

Surface Preparation

Components are prepared and cleaned to create the right foundation for a high-quality coating.

STEP 02

Pre-Treatment

Surface conditioning supports coating adhesion and helps maintain consistency throughout the process.

STEP 03

PVD Coating

The selected finish is applied using a controlled physical vapour deposition coating process.

STEP 04

Quality Inspection

The finished surface is checked for appearance and coating consistency before delivery.
